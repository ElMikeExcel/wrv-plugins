### #5 — the truncation measurement (the number Mike asked this rig for)

Mechanism (Magic source, CasterProperties.updateMaxMana): effective max
and regen are `(int)(base × (1 + boost))` — **truncated to whole numbers**.
Measured on the rig beginner path (base pool 50, base regen 5; read via
`getEffectiveManaMax()` / `getEffectiveManaRegeneration()`):

| Magic Level | Boost | Eff. pool (of 50) | Eff. regen (of 5) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | +1.25% | **50 (+0)** | **5 (+0)** |
| 3 | +2.5% | 51 (+1) | 5 (+0) |
| 5 | +5% | 52 (+2) | 5 (+0) |
| 9 | +10% | 55 (+5) | 5 (+0) |
| 10 | +11.25% | 55 (+5) | 5 (+0) |
| 15 | +17.5% | 58 (+8) | 5 (+0) |
| 20 | +23.75% | 61 (+11) | **6 (+1)** |

Derived for the LIVE beginner path (base 60/5, same proven formula):
pool 60 → L2 **60 (+0)**, L3 61, L5 63, L10 66, L20 74; regen identical
to the rig column (base 5): **zero regen gain until level 20 itself**.
Student path (100/15) for contrast: regen 15 → 15 (L5), 16 (L10), 18
(L20) — regen becomes real from student up, as SPEC §7.4 predicted. The
sharper finding the spec did NOT predict: **at level 2 — the first
level-up a new player ever sees — a beginner-path player gains literally
nothing measurable** (pool truncates to the same integer, regen
truncates, only the −0.5% cost reduction is real). The fix shape is the
one §7.4 names (front-load the table), and it is a CONFIG decision —
which is exactly why it goes to Mike with the level-count question
rather than being silently redesigned here.
