## Rig proof (Paper 26.3-235, Magic 11.2.5, Jobs + CMILib + Vault; log: rig/boot-L1-console.log)

Driver: `rig/driver/` (PartyRepairRig, rig-only, never shipped) — five fake players
(RigDriver2's NMS pattern) on one platform, same damaged kit (helmet 100, chestplate
150, leggings 120, boots 80, sword 200, offhand shield 90). Party formed through the
real PartyManager (`adopt`). **All verdicts are DURABILITY DELTAS**, per the study's
warning — not cast results.

Registration timing (the open design risk) resolved in favor of onLoad: the boot log
shows `[ExcelTogether] ExcelTogether: party-repair Magic actions registered
(ExcelPartyRepair, ExcelRepairItem).` in the load phase, the awake line ends
`Magic party repair: ON (ExcelPartyRepair + ExcelRepairItem registered).`, and Magic
loads the Flavor B spell with no error.

- **PARTY-CASTER-REPAIRED PASS** — caster 100/150/120/80/200 -> 0/0/0/0/0, offhand stays 90.
- **PARTY-ALLY-REPAIRED PASS** — ally identical deltas to 0, offhand stays 90.
- **STRANGER-UNTOUCHED PASS** — non-party player in the same spot: all six values unchanged.
- Together's own cast summary in the log: `ExcelTogether party repair: RigCaster
  repaired 10 item(s) across 2 party member(s).`
- **RECAST-REFUSED PASS** — immediate recast returned false; remaining cooldown
  **14,395,119 ms** (the 4-hour cooldown, engine-enforced).
- **SOLO-REPAIRED PASS** — a party-less caster's cast repairs his own 5 items
  (`repaired 5 item(s) across 1 party member(s)`), offhand untouched.
- **WHIFF finding** — RigWhiff (pristine kit) cast: the action repaired nothing and
  said so (`ExcelTogether party repair: RigWhiff cast Repair Equipment - nothing in
  the party needed repair.`), the cast itself returned true, and the recast probe
  shows **remaining 14,397,975 ms — a total whiff burns the full 4-hour cooldown.**
  This matches the study's boot-4 result for the stock spell and is structural:
  Magic charges the cooldown when the spell casts, the spell casts because its
  target set (the party) is non-empty, and the action API offers no post-hoc veto.
  Reported plainly, per the brief; no engine hack attempted.

## Fail-soft legs

- **L2 — no Magic** (log: rig/boot-L2-console.log): ExcelTogether 0.5.1 enables fully;
  awake line ends `Magic party repair: OFF (Magic not installed).`; server reaches
  Done; no Together errors. (The only ERROR lines are Jobs' own "Vault economy
  missing" rig noise — no economy plugin exists in the rig; unrelated to 0.5.1.)
- **L3 — Magic + Flavor B spells, no Together jar** (log: rig/boot-L3-console.log):
  Magic logs exactly one scoped WARN — `Error loading class ExcelPartyRepair for
  spell Repair Equipment` / `Failed to resolve class: ExcelPartyRepair` — loads its
  spells and reaches Done. So a spells-before-jar order degrades one spell with one
  warning, never the server. The packets still sequence jar-first.
