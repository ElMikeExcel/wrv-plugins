# ExcelMenu 2.0 — Phase 2 (the big menu tidy-up)

**What this brings:** the server now runs ONE menu plugin instead
of two. Everything you tap works the same on Java and on Bedrock
(phone/console) — and the teleport menu can finally ask someone to
come to YOU.

## Teleport to a player — now with a direction choice

1. Open the menu (compass or `/menu`) and tap **Teleport to a
   player** — or just type `/tpmenu`.
2. Tap the player you mean.
3. Choose: **Teleport to player** (you travel to them) or **Ask
   player to come to me** (they travel to you).
4. They get a request popup: **Accept** or **Decline**. Nothing
   happens until they accept, and closing the choice sends nothing.

On Java the same steps use a chest of player heads, then a small
choice chest; the request arrives as clickable [Accept] / [Decline]
in chat.

## The Halloween Scares button works again

Tapping **Halloween Scares — your switches** in the menu had
stopped opening anything. It now opens your scare switches again
(pumpkin hat, Slenderman, Herobrine and the rest), on both
platforms. The event still runs through Nov 5.

## Ask the Guide on Bedrock gets its text box back

Tap **Ask the Guide** in the menu and a text box opens: type your
question, tap Send, and the Guide answers from the server's own
pages. (Typing `/ask` and your question in chat still works too.)

## Skills & Perks on Bedrock

Tap **Skills & Perks** for the form again: your seal progress in
the Magic line, a **My mcMMO stats** button, and **Skill Trees on
the Website** for every ladder.

## Server Commands

`/servercommands` opens the full catalogue menu. Want just one
group in chat? Add its name or number, like
`/servercommands claims` or `/servercommands 5`.
`/serverwebsite` still shows the website address — clickable on
Java, spelled out (and copied to chat) on Bedrock.

## What did NOT change

Your compass, `/menu`, the shop menus, Spawn/Guild/Home confirm
popups (same wording, same Yes/Cancel), parties, and every other
menu look and work exactly as before. This update is the plumbing
underneath: one plugin, both platforms, no features lost.
