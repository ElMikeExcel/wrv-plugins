## What was built, per defect

### 1. Pager buttons (new `PagerHook` + `core/PagerPlan`)
While a Tap bar / Tap to pick player's spell bar is open in their own
inventory screen, grid slots **34 (Back) and 35 (Next)** hold pager buttons
— the settled design's "last two bottom-right slots". The forward button's
lore carries **"Page X of Y"**; a bare tap flips via `MagicHook.flipPage`
(the same wraparound as the wand click, which is untouched and stays).
Buttons are PDC-marked with their page state, render only when the wand
has 2+ pages, and are swept (marker-checked only) when the bar closes.
Hard protection, two layers: ArrangeHook's four interpreters return early
on any event touching a button slot, and PagerHook cancels every such
click/drag itself — a button can never be lifted, carried, counted as a
spell, or written. A place attempt carrying an icon is stopped but does
**not** flip (only a bare tap flips). A foreign item in a button slot is
never overwritten — that button simply doesn't render (counted in the
pager summary). Poll-based rendering (10 ticks) re-renders after any page
change, including Magic's own repaints wiping the slots mid-flip.

### 2. Two-tap write fix (`ArrangeHook`, plan pinned by DragWritePlan)
Root cause, from the code: a put is a two-gesture sequence whose terminal
click was judged against the *full* drag context — including the bar being
open at that instant. A repaint (a commit's, a cooldown icon's) can close
the bar's screen state between the taps; the place then fell through to
vanilla, the virtual icon physically moved, and the next repaint snapped
it back. Removes are a single gesture, so they always landed — exactly
Mike's report. Three changes:
- **Commit context:** a gesture already in flight (recorded lift, or an
  icon on the cursor) keeps its terminal commit under a lighter context —
  drag-write on, unarmed, Tap bar style, still in the player's own screen —
  never the bar's momentary open state.
- **Lift rescue:** two ticks after a pickup, if the bar died mid-gesture,
  it is reopened at once so the place lands in a live presentation.
- **Hold-read:** a pending lift is cancelled only when the read form
  actually opens (0.3.7 cancelled unconditionally, so a ≥800 ms pause
  between taps on Bedrock silently killed the gesture whenever the read
  couldn't open its page).
Drag-off-to-remove is unchanged (layout only, spell stays learned).

### 3. Login wand-mode trap (`TapBarHook` join guard + `core/JoinGuardPlan`)
On join, checks run at 30/80/160 ticks: a spell bar open inside the grace
window with no deliberate engagement since the join is a *restored* bar —
the guard closes it with Magic's own close (items restored) and logs
`Join guard: <name> logged in with the spell bar open; items restored
(joins start on your items)`, tells the player one gray line, and
schedules a CompassGuard tidy (a compass handed out at join can now be
deduped against the restored inventory — WandBars never gives, eats, or
duplicates compasses; CompassGuard's existing dedupe does the tidy).
Engagement = a slot pick or a bar open stamped after the first-second
churn window (the wand-slot sweep stamps picks server-side at join, so
stamps inside that window prove nothing). The exit is hotbar-level and
slot-independent by construction, so nothing parked in the exit slot can
block it.

## Design decisions made inside the brief's latitude
1. **"Spell inventory" = the player's own inventory screen while the bar
   is open** (the surface drag-write and Arrange already own), and the
   buttons flip **wand hotbar pages** — the pages Mike paged through in
   the report ("Page 2 of 3"). Grid slots 34/35 are the bottom-right pair
   of that screen, as the design note specifies.
2. **Buttons render only at 2+ pages** (one page = nowhere to flip; the
   two slots stay the player's).
3. **Flip gesture = a bare tap** (empty cursor). Carrying taps are treated
   as (blocked) place attempts, not page flips.
4. **Join grace = 8 s** with checks at 1.5 s / 4 s / 8 s, and the
   engagement exemption uses a 1 s post-join churn window. All three
   values are config keys (`tap-bar.join-starts-on-items`,
   `tap-bar.join-grace-ms`, `tap-bar.pager-buttons.enabled`), honored from
   code defaults — the live config does not need the keys added.
5. The join guard covers the **Tap bar / Tap to pick** styles (the
   opens-on-hold family the trap was reported on); Classic/Sneak bars
   joins are untouched.
