**Soak — PASSED with the overload policy firing as designed.** Burst 1: 10,000 publish-door records in 270 ms; the items stream (2nd in the shed order, effective cap ≈6,363) **shed 3,637 — and wrote the marker**: `overload_shed stream=items shed=3637 dropped_total=3637` in the system stream; status counters agree. Nothing was silently lost. Burst 2 (queue drained): 5,000 in 37 ms, **all 5,000 landed** (total 11,363). Max 100-record batch on the main thread: 52 ms during class-load warmup, ~3–16 ms steady. TPS during soak ≈ 19.9 (fresh-boot rig average). Handler cost is one enqueue per record; serialization is bounded per event.

**Restart / commit window — measured, stated honestly.** Graceful stop (SIGTERM): a 3,000-record burst fired seconds before shutdown **survived 100%** (14,363 total) via the onDisable flush. SIGKILL with `flush-interval-ms` raised to 30,000 for determinism: a 2,000-record burst was **lost in full** (count unchanged at 17,363) — the commit window is exactly the flush interval (default 1,000 ms) plus nothing else; lines flushed are durable, lines queued are not. No corruption in any restart (files reopen and append cleanly).
