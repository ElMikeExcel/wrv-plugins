- `requestCommand(TELEPORT_TO, "Jester_Turtle")` = `"tpa Jester_Turtle"`.
- `requestCommand(COME_TO_ME, "Jester_Turtle")` = `"tpahere Jester_Turtle"`.

- `promptText(COME_TO_ME, "Mike")` = `"Mike is asking you to teleport to them."`

The direction→command mapping and both prompt strings are pure static
helpers on `TeleportMenu` in the same shape the suite already uses for
`ExcelBedrockMenu`'s helpers, so the values players will actually see are
the values under test.
