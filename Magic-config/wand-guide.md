# How to use your wand (Wink Reyes Valley)

Your first wand knows Healer spells: Heal, Boon, Feed, Extinguish and Aqualung. Cure comes later when you rank up to Student.

## Java (mouse and keyboard)
- Cast: hold the wand and left-click.
- Switch spell: right-click. A spell chest opens. Click the spell you want.
- Quick switch: sneak (Shift) and right-click to jump to the next spell.
- See your spell: its name shows on the wand while you hold it.

## Bedrock touch (phone or tablet)
- Cast: hold the wand and tap the air or a mob.
- Switch spell: press and hold on the screen. A spell chest opens. Tap the spell you want.
- See your spell: its name shows above your hotbar when you hold the wand.
- Tip: if press and hold breaks a block, look at the sky first.

## Bedrock controller (Xbox, also on iPad)
- Cast: hold the wand and press RT (right trigger).
- Switch spell: press LT (left trigger). A spell chest opens. Move to the spell and press A.
- Quick switch: crouch (press the right stick) and press LT for the next spell.
- See your spell: its name shows above your hotbar when you hold the wand.

Spells use mana. Wait a few seconds if a spell will not cast. The wand is bound to you, so you can't lose it.
