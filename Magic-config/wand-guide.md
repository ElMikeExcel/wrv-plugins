# How to use your wand (Wink Reyes Valley)

Your first wand knows Healer spells: Heal, Boon, Feed, Extinguish and Aqualung. Cure comes later when you rank up to Student.

## Java (mouse and keyboard)
- Cast: left-click.
- Next spell: right-click. It switches right away, no menu.
- Spell list: sneak (Shift) and right-click, then click a spell.
- Back one spell: press Q.
- See your spell: its name shows above your hotbar and on the wand.

## Bedrock touch (phone or tablet)
- Cast: tap the air or a mob.
- Next spell: press and hold on the screen. It switches right away.
- Spell list: crouch, then press and hold. Tap a spell.
- See your spell: its name shows above your hotbar.
- Tip: if press and hold breaks a block, look at the sky first.

## Bedrock controller (Xbox, also on iPad)
- Cast: press RT (right trigger).
- Next spell: press LT (left trigger). It switches right away.
- Spell list: crouch (press the right stick) and press LT. Move to a spell and press A.
- See your spell: its name shows above your hotbar.

Spells use mana. Wait a few seconds if a spell will not cast. The wand is bound to you, so you can't lose it.
