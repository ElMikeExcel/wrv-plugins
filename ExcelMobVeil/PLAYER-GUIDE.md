# Veil Potion

**$2,000 per potion. Every drink adds eight real-time minutes.**

Open your compass menu → Spawn Shop → **Veil Potion** (or use `/veil buy`), review the price and balance, then Confirm to receive one potion. Buying it does nothing until you drink it. Drink another to add eight minutes to whatever remains; two fresh drinks give about sixteen minutes. Use `/veil status` to check your time.

Your body becomes invisible and standard mobs ignore you, even in armor. Other players can still see your armor and held items. Harvesting, planting, tilling, chopping and mining keep the effect. Hitting an animal, mob, player or other entity, or using an offensive projectile, ends all accumulated time and reveals you. Passive Thorns does not count as an attack.

Milk and death end all time too. Time continues while offline and through restarts. Do not brew Veil Potions; brewing removes their custom properties.

You can still take damage. Scripted bosses, area attacks, existing projectiles and environmental hazards are not blocked. The first version does not promise full EliteMobs/dragon coverage. This is separate from Death Protection.
