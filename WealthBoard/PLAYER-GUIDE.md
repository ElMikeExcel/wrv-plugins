# ExcelWealthBoard (WealthBoard) - player instructions

**Display name:** ExcelWealthBoard
**Installed plugin name:** WealthBoard
**Category:** Created by us
**Current installed version:** 1.3.0-mobile.6 (October 13, 2026)

## What happens when you join

About 10 seconds after you get into the world, a welcome popup appears:

- Title: **Wink Reyes Valley**
- Underneath it, the three richest players take turns, one at a time,
  about 3 seconds each, with their full name and balance, for example
  `Richest: 1. GumGumJetPistol $6,481.24`.

The popup starts a little after you join on purpose: titles sent while
your game is still loading never appear, especially on phones.

If nobody has a balance yet, the popup says
**No balances yet - go earn!**

## Java and Bedrock

The popup looks the same on Java (PC) and Bedrock (phone, tablet,
console). The one-at-a-time design is what makes it fit phone screens:
all three rankings on one line ran off the edges, and extra subtitle
lines do not appear on Bedrock.

There is no permanent sidebar.

## Commands

- `/leaderboard` - reopen the full wealth and mcMMO boards at any time
- `/baltop` - the wealth rankings on their own

## Earning money

You are enrolled in every Job automatically when you join, so you earn
money and XP from ordinary play. There is nothing to sign up for.
