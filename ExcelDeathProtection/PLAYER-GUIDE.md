# Death Protection Potion

The Spawn Shop sells a **Death Protection Potion** for **$15,000**.

## What it does

1. Buy the potion at the Spawn Shop (tap it, then tap again to confirm). The price and confirmation also appear as a big message on your screen.
2. Drink it. You will see **☠✔ Items protected is now ACTIVE**, and a small blue shell icon appears by your hearts. This is Conduit Power, Minecraft's effect for underwater breathing, clearer underwater vision, and faster mining. It is also listed when you open your inventory.
3. While that icon is there, you are protected: **if you die, you keep all of your items.** You still die and respawn as normal, and the protection is then used up.

## What it does NOT do

- It does **not** stop you from dying.
- It does **not** save your XP levels. Your XP drops like a normal death.
- One potion protects you for **one** death. After it saves you, it is gone.
- Items with Curse of Vanishing still vanish when you die. That is a normal game rule.

## Good to know

- The protection stays on when you log out and back in. It only ends when it saves you.
- While protection is on, you also get **Conduit Power**: underwater breathing, clearer underwater vision, and faster mining. The mining boost also works on land.
- Drinking a second potion while you are already protected does nothing, and the potion is not used up.
- Check yourself any time with `/dp status`.
