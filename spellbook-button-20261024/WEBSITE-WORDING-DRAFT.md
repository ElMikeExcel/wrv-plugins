# Website wording draft (for "How to use your wand" and the compass menu section). Draft only, not merged.

**Spellbook.** Open the compass menu and tap Spellbook to see every spell on your Magic
wand: what it costs, its cooldown, its range and its rank. It works while you hold the
compass, as long as your wand is in your inventory (not in your Utility Pouch). You can
also type /spellbook.

**Wand controls: Classic or Sneak bars.** Everyone starts on Classic: right-click (on a
phone or tablet, press and hold; on a controller, LT) picks the next spell, and
left-click casts. Sneak + right-click opens your spell list. Bedrock players (phone,
tablet, console) can switch to Sneak bars: right-click opens your spell bar, tap a spell
to cast it, and press sneak to flip to the next bar. Pick with the Wand controls button
in the compass menu or type /wandstyle. You can switch back any time. Java players use
Classic.

**Wand tip.** For your first few wand uses, a short tip explains the two styles. Tap OK
(or type /wandstyle ok) and it won't show again.

**Auto-sort my chests.** Tap "Auto-sort my chests: on/off" in the compass menu (or type
/autosort) to turn it on or off just for you. When it's on, your chests, barrels, shulker
boxes and ender chest tidy themselves when you open and close them. It's off until you
turn it on.
